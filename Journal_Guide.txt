Here are some good vocabulary and phrases to make your writing
more academic:
1. 	Academic Connectors and Transitions
• 	Furthermore: To add more information.
• 	Moreover: Used to introduce an additional point in a persuasive argument.
• 	Nevertheless/Nonetheless: To introduce a contrasting idea.
• 	Consequently: To show a cause-effect relationship.
• 	Hence/Thus/Therefore: To express results or conclusions.
• 	In contrast: When comparing differing viewpoints or data.
• 	On the other hand: To present an alternative perspective.
• 	Despite this: To acknowledge an opposing fact.
2. Conclusion and Summary Phrases
• 	In conclusion/To conclude: To signal the end of an argument.
• 	To summarize/In summary: To condense the key points.
• 	In essence: To distill the core idea.
• 	Ultimately: Indicates the final, most important point or outcome.

A good test plan should be written scientifically, it should be written in a way
that supports repetition and testing by others. Explain two steps you will follow
in designing your test plan? (EDP)
In working on any project, it’s important to follow the EDP. The EDP is an abbre-
viation for the Engineering design process. It’s a process that includes several steps
to follow while solving any problem and designing projects. After following the
engineering design process, we have come to the phase where we test our prototype
to make sure that it’s working efficiently. In this phase, we test the chosen design
requirements. These requirements include producing 150 joules of energy in at
most 5 minutes and being user friendly so that anyone can use it. There are some
steps that we followed to achieve a good test plan:
1. 	Since we are required to generate electricity with specific amount (150 joules
in 5 minutes) we made sure that the devices we use in the test plan are accurate
and will give us accurate measurements to use. These devices include voltmeter
and ammeter. This will contribute to making the process of testing viable for
anyone.
2. 	Dealing with electricity means being in danger. Since there are many conse-
quences that may happen while testing our prototype and measuring the elec-
tricity it produces, we made sure that we follow the right safety precautions. This
includes keeping water away from the prototype and paying attention to the
wires that conduct electricity. This will lead to safer and easier testing for the pro-
totype.
It’s important to highlight that we made our test plan accurate, safe, and user friend-
ly as much as possible.
