Using the Engineering Design Process (EDP)
Water-land ratio is constant (75%), but the pollution of water Is duplicating every year, to

water impurities. In our solution we depended on different abilities of natural and
recycled materials in reducing water impurities such as cotton and sand that is able to

During constructing our prototype, some negative issues have face us but the most

inefficient or can be toxic to human health, some examples are bentonite clay and egg

human conditions.

a sufficient electric current to make our solution that will be able to convert the waves of
sound into electricity by using a transducer that has a special ability in absorb the
surrounding vibrations and generate electricity from it. This solution was liked by all the
